# Why Morph Lives Under Levizr

**Part of:** [The Story of Morph](index.md)

People ask this when they see the repo: *what is Levizr? Is Levizr funding this project?* Honest answers — and one of them is "no."

## Is Levizr funding this project?

**No.**

Levizr is me. I don't have a company funding me — I'm the entire company, the entire team, and the entire budget. There's no investor, no sponsor, no organization behind Morph. Just me and my i5.

## So what is Levizr, actually?

Levizr is **my own** — I use it as my company. It's the name under which Morph and the rest of my work lives. You can say it's a company, in the sense that I'm the founder and it's the label for my work.

I founded it on **1 January 2025**. If you want proof, look at the domain registry — `levizr.com` is registered on 1 January 2025.

So I just did it. No powerful reason, no grand business plan — I wanted a name to put my work under, and I made it official that day.

## Why "Levizr"?

The same answer as *why Morph is Morph*: **I found a word nobody was using.**

It's unique. I searched, it wasn't taken — not by a company, not by a project, not by anything meaning the same thing. And that's rare enough in 2025 that I kept it.

That's the whole story. Levizr isn't a mystery, isn't a big corporation, isn't a funder — it's a name I found, a domain I registered on 1 January 2025, and the label on my work. Including this one.
